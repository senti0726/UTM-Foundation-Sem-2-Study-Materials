Law of thermodynamics $\implies \Delta Q=\Delta U+W$
$\underline{\text{Isometric change}}$
$\Delta Q=\Delta U+W$
$V$ is constant
$W=p\Delta V$
$\;\;\;\;\;=p(0)$
$W=0$
$\Delta Q=\Delta U=nC_\text{rim}\Delta T$
$nC_\text{rim}\Delta T=n \frac{t}{2}R\Delta T$
$\;C_\text{rim}=\frac{t}{2}R-Q$

$\underline{\text{Isotonic change}}$
$\Delta Q=\Delta U+W$
$P$ is constant
$\Delta Q=\Delta U+p\Delta V$
$nC_\text{pim}\Delta T=nC_\text{vim}\Delta T+nR\Delta T$
$C_\text{pim}=C_\text{vim}+R$
$C_\text{pim}-C_\text{vim}=R$
  $C_\text{pim}\;>\;C_\text{vim}$
$\;\;\;\;\;\downarrow$                   $\searrow$
$\uparrow$ in $\Delta U$ and $W$       $\uparrow$ in $\Delta V$ only
$C_\text{pim}=n \frac{t}{2}R$
