# Chapter 4

> [!formula]- Autoionization of Water
> $\ce{2H2O(l) <=> H3O+(aq) + OH-(aq)}$ 
> or simplified: $\ce{H2O(l) <=> H+(aq) + OH-(aq)}$

> [!formula]- pH and pOH
> > [!continue]- **pH**
> > $pH = -\log[\ce{H+}]$
> 
> > [!continue]- **pOH**
> > $pOH = -\log[\ce{OH-}]$
> 
> > [!continue]- **Relationship**
> > $pH + pOH = 14$

> [!formula]- Acid-Base Constants
> > [!continue]- **Weak Acid Dissociation**
> > $\ce{HA + H2O <=> H3O+ + A-}$
> 
> > [!continue]- **Weak Base Dissociation**
> > $\ce{B + H2O <=> BH+ + OH-}$
> 
> > [!continue]- **Relationship ($K_a$ and $K_b$)**
> > $K_a \times K_b = K_w$
> 
> > [!continue]- **Logarithmic Form**
> > $pK_a + pK_b = pK_w = 14$ (at $25^{\circ}C$)

> [!formula]- Ionization and Hydrolysis
> > [!continue]- **Percent Ionization**
> > $\% \text{ Ionization} = \frac{\text{conc. ionized}}{\text{initial conc.}} \times 100\%$
> 
> > [!continue]- **Percent Hydrolysis**
> > $\% \text{ Hydrolysis} = \frac{[\text{Ions}]_{\text{Hydrolysed}}}{[\text{Ions}]_{\text{Initial}}} \times 100\%$

> [!formula]- Buffer Solutions (Henderson-Hasselbalch)
> $pH = pK_a + \log \frac{[\text{base species}]}{[\text{acid species}]}$

> [!formula]- Titration Calculations
> > [!continue]- **Initial pH (Weak Acid)**
> > $[\ce{H3O+}] = \sqrt{K_a \times [\ce{HA}]_{\text{init}}}$
> 
> > [!continue]- **Buffer Region**
> > $pH = pK_a + \log \left( \frac{[\text{base}]}{[\text{acid}]} \right)$
> 
> > [!continue]- **Equivalence Point (Weak Acid + Strong Base)**
> > $[\ce{OH-}] = \sqrt{K_b \times [\ce{A-}]}$ where $[\ce{A-}] = \frac{mol\ \ce{HA}_{\text{init}}}{V_{\text{acid}} + V_{\text{base}}}$

> [!formula]- Electrochemistry
> > [!continue]- **Standard Notation**
> > $\text{Anode} \mid \text{Anode Electrolyte} \parallel \text{Cathode Electrolyte} \mid \text{Cathode}$
> 
> > [!continue]- **Cell Potential**
> > $E^\circ_{\text{cell}} = E^\circ_{\text{red}}(\text{cathode}) - E^\circ_{\text{red}}(\text{anode})$
> 
> > [!continue]- **Nernst Equation**
> > $E_{\text{cell}} = E^\circ_{\text{cell}} - \frac{RT}{nF} \ln Q$
