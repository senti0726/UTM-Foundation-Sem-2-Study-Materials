1. $a)$ The weight of palm oils brought to the factory after being harvested was recorded and displayed in the cumulative frequency table below:

| Weight (tonnes) | Frequency |
| :---: | :---: |
| 10-14 | 6 |
| 15-19 | 12 |
| 20-24 | 17 |
| 25-29 | 24 |
| 30-34 | 13 |
| 35-39 | 8 |

   $i.$ Calculate the mean, mode and median for the above data.
   $ii.$ Find the maximum weights of palm oils brought to the factory in the lower 40%.

   $b)$ Given that the mean and standard deviation of a sample of 50 data are 4.10 and 1.93 respectively. Find the value of $\sum x_{i}^{2}$.


2. $a)$ A sport tournament is participated by 32 countries. After finishing the first round, only 16 countries will qualify to the second round.
   $i.$ In how many ways can these countries qualify to the second round?
   $ii.$ If 5 countries have already qualified to the second round, find the number of ways can the remaining countries qualify to the second round.

   $b)$ 5 friends are going on a trip by car. The car has 5 seats including the driver's seat.
   $i.$ Find the number of sit arrangements if all person can sit anywhere including the driver's seat.
   $ii.$ If only 2 persons able to drive the car, how many sit arrangements are possible.

   $c)$ Consider the letters of the word 'BOOLE'. Find the number of different arrangements for the following conditions:
   $i.$ No conditions.
   $ii.$ The letter 'O' not always together.

   $i.$ Are students having iPhone and students having iPad independent?
   $ii.$ What is the probability that a student neither has iPhone nor iPad?

   $b)$ A company uses three local hostels A, B and C to provide accommodations for its employees. It is known that 20% of the employees are assigned rooms at Hostel A, 45% at Hostel B and 35% at Hostel C. It is also known that the probabilities of Hostel A, Hostel B and Hostel C are not facing water disruption are 0.95, 0.90 and 0.97 respectively.
   $i.$ Construct a tree diagram to illustrate the above data.    $ii.$ What is the probability that an employee is assigned to a hostel not facing water disruption? Hence, find the probability that an employee is facing water disruption.
   $iii.$ Given that the hostel is facing water disruption, what is the probability that it is at Hotel B?


4. A continuous random variable $X$ has the following probability density function:
   $f(x) = \begin{cases} \frac{2}{7}, & 0 \le x < 1 \\ \frac{2c}{3}, & 1 \le x < 5 \\ 0, & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$

   $a)$ Show that $c=\frac{2}{7}$.

   $b)$ Compute $P(0.5 < X < 2.5)$.

   $c)$ Calculate the mean and variance of $X$.

   $d)$ If $Y=3X+1$, find $E(Y)$ and $Var(Y)$.


5. Past records indicate that in a large restaurant, 3 out of 5 customers ask for water with their meal.
   $a)$ If a random sample 10 of customers is selected, find the probability that
   $i.$ exactly 6 customers ask for water with their meal.
   $ii.$ less than 9 customers ask for water with their meal.
   $iii.$ between 3 and 8 customers do not ask for water with their meal.

   $b)$ If the probability that no customer from the random sample of $m$ customers do not ask for water is 0.13, find the value of $m$.

7. $a)$ A company produces light bulbs with a lifespan that follows a normal distribution with a mean of 800 hours and a variance of 38000 hours.
   $i.$ What is the probability that a randomly selected light bulb has a lifespan greater than 550 hours?
   $ii.$ Find the probability that a randomly selected light bulb has a lifespan between 600 and 700 hours.
   $iii.$ Hence, determine the expected number of light bulbs with a lifespan between 600 and 700 hours when 300 units of light bulbs are randomly selected.

   $b)$ The volume of perfume production by a factory is normally distributed with a mean of $20\,m^{3}$ and a standard deviation of $\sigma\,m^{3}$. If the probability that less than $5\,m^{3}$ of perfume produced is 0.4, Find the value of $\sigma$.

   $ii.$ the probability that between 745 and 778 patients will be cured.

   $ii.$ Find the probability that in the next 15 weeks, the estate agent sells between 10 and 30 houses.
